### COVID-19 Overview
COVID-19 (Coronavirus Disease 2019) is an infectious disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus. It primarily spreads through respiratory droplets when an infected person coughs, sneezes, or talks.

### Common Symptoms
The most common symptoms of COVID-19 include:
- Fever or chills
- Cough
- Shortness of breath or difficulty breathing
- Fatigue
- Muscle or body aches
- Headache
- New loss of taste or smell
- Sore throat
- Congestion or runny nose
- Nausea or vomiting
- Diarrhea

Symptoms may appear 2-14 days after exposure to the virus. Anyone can have mild to severe symptoms. Older adults and people who have severe underlying medical conditions like heart or lung disease or diabetes seem to be at higher risk for developing more serious complications.

### Treatment and Management
For mild symptoms:
- Rest and stay hydrated.
- Take over-the-counter medicines, such as acetaminophen or ibuprofen, to relieve fever and pain.
- Isolate at home to prevent spreading the virus to others.

For severe cases, medical treatments may include:
- Antiviral medications (e.g., Paxlovid, Remdesivir) which help the body fight the virus.
- Monoclonal antibodies to block the virus from entering cells.
- Corticosteroids (like Dexamethasone) to reduce inflammation.
- Oxygen therapy or ventilators to support breathing in extreme cases.

### Prevention
- Get vaccinated and stay up to date with COVID-19 boosters.
- Wear a well-fitting mask in crowded indoor spaces.
- Wash hands frequently with soap and water for at least 20 seconds.
- Maintain physical distance from others, especially those who are sick.
